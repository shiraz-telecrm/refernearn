FAQ

Referral questions, answered clearly.

Who can refer?

Any existing TeleCRM customer can refer a business that may benefit from TeleCRM.

How much do I earn?

You earn 10% of the referred company's first payment after they become a customer.

How do I get paid?

The TeleCRM team will confirm the eligible referral and coordinate your payout details.

Can I choose credits instead of cash?

Yes. You can choose account credits and adjust the reward against your TeleCRM subscription.

Is there a referral limit?

No. You can refer as many relevant businesses as you want.

What if the company is already talking to TeleCRM?

Our team will verify the lead status and let you know whether the referral is eligible.
