Common questions

Everything you'd want to know

Do I need to be an active telecrm customer to refer?

Yes — the Refer & Earn program is exclusively for current telecrm subscribers. If you're actively paying for a telecrm plan, you're eligible to refer.

How much do I earn per referral?

You earn 10% of your referral's first payment. So if they sign up 10 users on the ₹799/user/month annual plan, their first payment is ₹95,880 — and you earn ₹9,588, either as cash or credits.

Can I choose cash for some referrals and credits for others?

Yes. You choose your preferred reward type per referral at the time of submission. You don't have to pick one format for everything.

When do I get paid?

Cash is transferred within 7 business days of your referral's first payment clearing. Credits are applied to your account in the same billing cycle. There's no waiting period or minimum payout threshold.

What if the business I referred was already in contact with telecrm?

If the referred business is already in our CRM as a lead or existing contact, the referral may not qualify. We'll let you know within 24 hours of submission. When in doubt, submit anyway — we'll confirm.

Is there a limit on how many referrals I can make?

No limit. Refer 1 or 50 — every qualifying referral earns you 10%. There are no caps, quotas, or exclusivity clauses.

What happens if my referral cancels before their first payment?

The reward is based on the referral's first actual payment. If they don't convert, there's no payout for that referral — but your other referrals are unaffected.

Terms & Conditions

Simple rules for eligible rewards

Referral rewards are subject to TeleCRM verification and program terms.

Rewards apply only to eligible new customers and are calculated as 10% of the first payment received by TeleCRM, excluding taxes and adjustments.

Eligibility

The program is open to existing TeleCRM customers with an active account. Employees, partners, vendors, agencies, and resellers may be excluded unless approved in writing.

Valid Referrals

A referral must be a new business that is not already a TeleCRM customer, active lead, demo prospect, or in discussion with the TeleCRM sales team.

Reward Calculation

The reward is 10% of the referred customer's first payment received by TeleCRM, excluding taxes, discounts, refunds, payment gateway charges, and other deductions.

Payout Options

Rewards may be paid as cash or issued as TeleCRM account credits, based on the selected preference, eligibility verification, and TeleCRM approval.

Payout Timing

Rewards become payable only after the referred business becomes a paid customer and TeleCRM receives the first payment. Eligible rewards are processed after internal verification.

Cancellations

If the referred customer cancels, receives a refund, disputes the payment, or fails verification, the referral reward may be cancelled or reversed.

Duplicate Referrals

If multiple people refer the same business, the reward will generally be assigned to the first eligible referrer recorded by TeleCRM.

Compliance

Applicable taxes, TDS, GST, or statutory deductions may apply. Referrers may need to provide PAN, GST details, bank details, invoice, or other documents before payout.

Misuse

TeleCRM may reject referrals, withhold rewards, suspend participation, or recover paid rewards in cases of fraud, self-referrals, fake businesses, misleading information, or abuse.

Program Changes

TeleCRM may modify, pause, or discontinue the referral program, reward percentage, eligibility rules, or payout method at any time. TeleCRM's decision will be final.
